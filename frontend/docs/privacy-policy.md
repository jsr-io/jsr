_Last updated: August 21, 2026_

This Privacy Notice explains how Deno Land Inc. (**Deno**, **we**, **us**, or
**our**) handles personal information in connection with the public package
registry at [jsr.io](https://jsr.io), its website, API, and related services
(together, **JSR**).

JSR is a public registry. Package contents, documentation, metadata, scope and
package names, and public publisher information can be viewed, downloaded,
cached, indexed, and mirrored by anyone. Do not publish secrets, confidential
information, or personal information that should not be public. We cannot recall
copies that other people have already obtained.

We seek to minimize personal information and collect, use, and retain
information reasonably necessary and proportionate to operate, secure, maintain,
and improve JSR and comply with law.

## Information we collect

Depending on how you use JSR, we collect the following categories of
information.

### Account and authentication information

When you sign in through an identity provider, we receive information needed to
create, authenticate, secure, and maintain your account. This may include your
name, username, email address, avatar, provider identifiers, public profile
information, and authentication information used to connect your account.

You may use a pseudonym. Some account information, such as your display name,
avatar, provider username, scope membership, and publishing activity, may be
publicly associated with your account.

### Published content and registry information

We collect the packages, source code, documentation, package metadata, scope and
package names, licenses, provenance, and other content you publish or provide.
We also maintain records connecting registry activity, such as publishing or
scope administration, to an account.

Published content and associated public metadata are public by design. They may
contain personal information if you or another publisher includes it.

### Usage, request, download, and device information

When you interact with JSR, we and the services operating on our behalf may
process information such as:

- IP address, user agent, request path, date and time, and response information;
- package searches, downloads, and aggregate usage statistics;
- device, browser, network, and approximate location information derived from an
  IP address; and
- reliability, performance, capacity, and diagnostic information.

JSR may store short-lived or otherwise limited information in your browser or on
the Service to maintain a session, complete authentication, remember your
preferences, protect against automated abuse, and keep the Service secure. JSR
does not use personal information for advertising or cross-site behavioral
profiling.

### Security, abuse, and audit information

We collect information needed to protect accounts and the software supply chain,
investigate abuse, enforce our policies, and keep a record of important registry
actions. This may include authentication events, security signals, account and
package activity, reports, investigation records, and audit logs.

We may inspect, analyze, and execute public package content to identify malware
or suspicious behavior, understand dependencies and compatibility, investigate
supply-chain incidents, and improve registry security and reliability.

### Communications

We collect information you provide in support requests, abuse reports, legal
notices, and other correspondence. This includes your contact details and the
contents and context of the communication.

We do not buy personal information from data brokers.

## How we use information

We use personal information to:

- provide, operate, maintain, and improve JSR;
- create, authenticate, secure, and administer accounts;
- publish, document, index, search, distribute, cache, and mirror packages;
- provide usage statistics and understand reliability, performance, and
  capacity;
- detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, malware, abuse, security incidents,
  and supply-chain threats;
- communicate about accounts, security, package activity, support requests,
  reports, and changes to JSR or its policies;
- enforce the [Terms of Service](/docs/terms-of-service) and
  [Usage Policy](/docs/usage-policy);
- protect JSR, its users, Deno, and others; and
- comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We may create and use aggregated or deidentified information for these purposes.
We do not attempt to reidentify information that we maintain as deidentified.

## When we disclose information

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising or cross-site
behavioral profiling. We disclose information only as reasonably necessary for
the following purposes.

### Public registry functionality

Information you publish or make public through JSR is available to anyone. It
may be redistributed through APIs, package archives, generated documentation,
caches, mirrors, search indexes, and other registry features. Other people may
retain that information independently of JSR.

### Service providers

Companies acting on our behalf process information to help operate JSR. Their
functions may include hosting and storage, content delivery and security,
search, authentication, transactional email, and observability. We provide them
only the information reasonably necessary for their work and require them to
protect it under appropriate contractual or legal obligations.

### Safety, rights, and legal process

We may disclose information where we reasonably believe it is necessary to
investigate or prevent abuse or a security incident; handle a package, name,
copyright, trademark, privacy, or other complaint; comply with law or valid
legal process; or protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users, the
public, Deno, or JSR. When appropriate, information in a report may be shared
with the affected publisher or other relevant parties.

### Change of control

We may disclose or transfer information in connection with a financing,
reorganization, merger, acquisition, sale of assets, insolvency, or similar
transaction. A recipient may use personal information only in a manner
consistent with this Notice unless it gives any notice or obtains any consent
required by law.

## Legal bases for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on one or more of the
following:

- **Contract:** processing needed to provide authenticated registry features
  under the [Terms of Service](/docs/terms-of-service).
- **Legitimate interests:** operating and improving a useful public registry;
  maintaining reliability and capacity; protecting users, JSR, and the software
  supply chain; investigating abuse; and communicating with you. We consider the
  nature of the information and the effect on your rights when relying on these
  interests.
- **Legal obligation:** processing needed to comply with law or legal process.
- **Consent:** processing for which we ask for consent. You may withdraw consent
  at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

## Retention and account deletion

We retain information for no longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes
described in this Notice, taking into account the nature and sensitivity of the
information, security and abuse risks, downstream reliance on packages,
applicable law, and potential legal claims.

In general:

- public packages and associated metadata are normally retained indefinitely
  because other people may depend on them, subject to exceptional removal;
- account information is retained while the account exists;
- authentication and browser state are retained for as long as needed to
  complete their functions and protect the Service;
- operational, request, security, abuse, audit, and correspondence records are
  retained for periods appropriate to their purpose and risk; and
- backups, caches, and mirrors expire through ordinary rotation or may persist
  independently where operated by other people.

You may request account deletion through account controls if available or by
contacting [help@jsr.io](mailto:help@jsr.io). When an account is deleted, we
delete or anonymize identifying account information after a limited retention
period reasonably necessary for security, fraud and abuse prevention,
supply-chain investigations, legal obligations, and claims. We may retain
records of significant account actions for those purposes while removing or
anonymizing their public attribution.

Account deletion does not ordinarily remove packages you published or
information that other people have copied. Package licenses may also require
attribution or permit others to preserve and redistribute copies. If published
content contains personal information that presents a privacy or security risk,
contact us so we can consider an exceptional removal. We cannot guarantee that
we can remove every copy or cached version.

We may preserve information for longer when required by law, legal process, or a
litigation or security hold. When the reason for preservation ends, the ordinary
retention criteria apply.

## Your choices and rights

You can choose not to create an account and can browse or download public
packages anonymously. You can change some public account information through
your identity provider or JSR account settings. You can control browser storage
through your browser, although blocking storage needed for authentication or
security may prevent some features from working.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to,
correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or to
restrict or object to certain processing. You may also have a right to appeal a
denied request or complain to a data protection authority. We may verify your
identity before acting on a request, and a request may be limited where another
person's rights or an applicable legal basis requires or permits us to retain or
use information. We will explain a denial where required by law.

To exercise a privacy right, email [help@jsr.io](mailto:help@jsr.io) with the
subject **Privacy Request**. Authorized agents may submit requests where
applicable law permits. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a
privacy right.

JSR does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral
advertising. We have not done so during the period covered by this Notice.

## Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to
protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely
secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please protect your account
credentials and report suspected compromise to
[help@jsr.io](mailto:help@jsr.io).

## International data transfers

Deno is based in the United States, and JSR and its service providers may
process information in the United States and other countries whose data
protection laws differ from those where you live. Where required, we use an
approved transfer mechanism or other safeguards for international transfers.

## Children

JSR accounts are not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child
under 16 has provided personal information through an account, contact
[help@jsr.io](mailto:help@jsr.io). We will investigate and take appropriate
action, including deleting the information where required by law.

## External services

JSR may link to websites and services that we do not control. Their privacy
practices are governed by their own notices, not this one.

## Changes to this Notice

We may update this Notice as JSR and its data practices evolve. We will post the
updated Notice with a new date. If a change materially affects how we use
personal information, we will provide additional notice where reasonably
practicable or required by law.
